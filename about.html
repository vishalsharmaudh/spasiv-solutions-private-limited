Who we are

How we started

SPAS IV Solutions started in 2026 when our founders were managing a construction site that couldn't find reliable labour on short notice.

The problem wasn't a lack of workers - it was a lack of verified ones. Half the people who showed up couldn't produce ID. Others vanished after the first pay cycle. Some were being paid less than agreed, with no paperwork to prove it.

So we built a system: document every worker, deploy them fast, pay them on time, and give businesses an audit trail they could actually rely on. That system became SPAS IV Solutions.

Today we operate across construction, warehousing, logistics, and technical trades - but the founding principle hasn't changed. We verify people properly, treat them fairly, and give businesses staff they can count on.
